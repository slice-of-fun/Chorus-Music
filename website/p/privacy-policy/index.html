Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Chorus Music. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you may provide while using our app.

1. Information We Collect

Chorus Music is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, listening history, or device information to our own servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The app interacts with third-party APIs (like YouTube Music, LRCLib, and others) to fetch music and lyrics. These services may log your IP address and search queries according to their own privacy policies.

3. Local Storage

Your settings, downloaded music, and listening history are stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data and can clear it at any time by clearing the app's cache or storage.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect contact information, please review this page periodically for any changes.
